# Chronicle Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Chronicle should be a place where people can do serious technical work without intimidation, harassment, or exclusion.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- give direct feedback without contempt;
- make room for questions and different levels of experience;
- respect privacy, boundaries, names, and pronouns;
- disclose conflicts of interest;
- correct mistakes and repair harm when possible;
- stop behavior when a moderator asks them to stop.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or sustained disruption;
- discriminatory or demeaning comments;
- sexualized attention or content in project spaces;
- publishing private information without permission;
- impersonation or deliberately misleading attribution;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern;
- using project access, status, or review authority to pressure others;
- knowingly exposing contributors to unsafe links, files, or credentials.

Strong disagreement is allowed. Bad-faith repetition, personal attacks, and status games are not substitutes for technical argument.

## Scope

This policy applies in Chronicle repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, meetings, chat spaces, events, and when someone officially represents the project.

## Reporting

For a private report, use the repository's [private reporting channel](https://github.com/ariostech/chronicle/security/advisories/new) and state that the report concerns community conduct rather than a software vulnerability. If the report concerns the person who receives that channel, contact Arios Technologies through an official public channel and request a private escalation path without describing sensitive details publicly.

Include the relevant links, dates, context, and the outcome you are seeking. Do not investigate other participants or gather private information yourself.

## Response

Project leaders will protect the reporter's privacy as far as reasonably possible, hear affected parties, examine available evidence, and avoid conflicts of interest. Reports will not be decided solely by popularity, contributor status, or employer.

Possible responses include:

1. clarification or a private request to change behavior;
2. a formal warning and defined conditions for continued participation;
3. temporary limits on participation or project access;
4. removal from a project role;
5. a permanent ban from project spaces.

The response should fit the conduct, impact, pattern, willingness to repair harm, and risk to the community. Urgent restrictions may be applied while a report is reviewed.

## Appeals

A person directly affected by a moderation decision may request one review by a disinterested project leader. An appeal must identify a process error, important missing evidence, or a disproportionate response. Disagreement alone is not grounds for repeated appeal.

## Attribution

This is a Chronicle-specific policy informed by common open-source community standards, including the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), but written for this project's governance and reporting paths.
